🏋️ My Weekly Fitness Routine 💪

Stay Healthy • Stay Strong • Stay Happy

Welcome! This weekly fitness planner helps maintain a healthy lifestyle through stretching, exercise, yoga, walking, and proper hydration. Every activity plays an important role in keeping the body active and energetic.

🌅 Morning Stretch

Start the day with simple stretches for your arms, legs, back, and neck.

⏰Time: 10 minutes

🏃 Cardio Workout

Do jumping jacks, jogging, or skipping to improve stamina and energy.

⏰Time: 20 minutes

🏋️ Strength Training

Practise squats, push-ups, and planks to build body strength.

⏰Time: 15 minutes

🧘 Yoga Time

Try simple yoga poses to improve balance, flexibility, and focus.

⏰Time: 15 minutes

🚶 Evening Walk

Go for a relaxing walk to stay active and refresh your mind.

⏰Time: 25 minutes

💧 Rest & Hydrate

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing your workout.

⏰Time: All day

💡 Fitness Tip

A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food,and consistency.
