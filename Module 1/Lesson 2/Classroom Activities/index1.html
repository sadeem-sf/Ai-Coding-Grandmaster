⚡ Fact 2: Lightning Strikes

Lightning can heat the air around it to hotter than the Sun’s surface.

How often does it strike?

Lightning happens thousands of times every minute around the world.

Can lightning strike twice?

Yes, tall buildings are often struck multiple times.
