Mini Biography: Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla was an inventor, engineer, and scientist who helped change the world through his work with electricity.

Early Life

He was born on 10 July 1856 in Smiljan, Croatia. From a young age, he loved learning and was known for his creative imagination.

Fun Fact: Tesla could remember entire books and visualize inventions in his mind.

Important Achievement

Tesla developed the Alternating Current (AC) power system, which is still used to deliver electricity around the world.

Many people consider him a visionary inventor because his ideas were far ahead of his time.

One of his most important contributions was making electricity more practical and efficient.

Correction Corner

Tesla invented electricity. He improved the way electricity could be generated and distributed.

Interesting Examples

Science uses symbols such as H2O and numbers like 102. Tesla's inventions helped advance many scientific fields.

He earned more than 300 patents during his lifetime.

Words That Inspire

Tesla once said, The future is mine.

The present is theirs; the future, for which I really worked, is mine.

My Reflection

I admire Nikola Tesla because he never gave up on his ideas. His creativity and determination continue to inspire inventors and students around the world.
