Why Your Body Needs Exercise?

Your body is like a supercar, the more you use it, the stronger it gets!KIds who move for 60 minutes daily are more likely to have stronger bones and muscles.Dancing,Playing and swimming are great ways to stay active and healthy.

Quick Tips for a Healthy Lifestyle

  • Eat colorful fruits and veggies every day!
  • Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.
  • Get at least 60 minutes of exercise daily.
  • Wash your hands regularly to keep germs away.
  • Get enough sleep to help your body grow strong.
