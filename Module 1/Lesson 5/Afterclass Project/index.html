💡Nutrition Tips for Kids

A balanced diet is essential for growing kids. Here are some tips:

  • 🍎Include a variety of fruits and vegetables in your meals.
  • 🥕Choose whole grains over refined grains.
  • 🍟Limit sugary drinks and snacks.
  • 🥤Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
  • 🍝Don't skip breakfast; it's the most important meal of the day!
  • 🍗Include lean proteins like chicken, fish, and beans in your diet.
