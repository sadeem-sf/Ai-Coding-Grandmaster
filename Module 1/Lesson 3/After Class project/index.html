My Dream School Timetable

In this webpage you will find the timetable for my dream school. I want my dream school to be like Harry Potter's Hogwarts School, but in my school they would not teach witchcraft. Instead, students would learn exciting and useful subjects while having fun every day.

The Insight of Hogwarts School

Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is a fictional school in the Harry Potter series. It is a magical school where young witches and wizards learn about magic, friendship, courage, and teamwork.

The school is divided into four houses:
Gryffindor
Hufflepuff
Ravenclaw
Slytherin

Each house has its own unique characteristics and values. Students take part in friendly competitions and earn points for their houses.

It does not do to dwell on dreams and forget to live.
